## Open-ended phase guide

Reconstructed from the questions actually sent to null (as a numbered list — see coaching note below; this should be bullets, not a script, going forward).

- **Current task-management method**
  - "Walk me through the last time you had a big assignment or test coming up — what did you actually do to keep track of it?"
- **Friction under heavy workload**
  - "Tell me about the last time you fell behind on schoolwork. What was going on right before that, and what did you do next?"
- **Current tools and dissatisfaction**
  - "What apps or systems have you tried for staying on top of schoolwork? What happened with each one — are you still using any of them?"
- **Streaks/rewards history** (tests whether null's novelty-decay finding generalizes)
  - "Have you ever used something with streaks or points — Duolingo, a fitness app, a game, whatever? What happened with it over time — did you stick with it or drop it?"
- **Abandonment pattern**
  - "Think of a tool or system you used for a while and then stopped. What made you stop?"

> **Coaching flag (from Interview 2):** two questions originally used with null were not open-ended/behavioral — "what would a tool need to do to keep you using it" (a feature question) and "would streaks make it easier" (a hypothetical naming the mechanic directly). Both got usable answers anyway, but the five areas above are the behavioral replacements — use these going forward instead.
